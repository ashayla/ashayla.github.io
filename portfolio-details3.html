Planetary Gear Assembly Trainer

A procedural VR assembly trainer that walks a learner through assembling a planetary gear system step by step, so they learn part relationships and sequence before touching real hardware.

Role

Overview

Unlike the equipment-operation modules, this project is built around sequence: the trainee assembles a planetary gear system piece by piece inside VR, with each step gated on the correct part and orientation.

That meant the UX work mattered as much as the 3D work — clear part call-outs, forgiving interaction tolerances, and feedback states that make it obvious when a part is placed correctly, all designed and tested for usability in Unity on Oculus.
