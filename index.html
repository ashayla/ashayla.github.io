About

Ashayla Williams

I build immersive worlds tailored to diverse learners — particularly in health, job training, and education.

My focus is virtual and augmented reality development for real‑world training: taking equipment most people never get hands‑on access to and rebuilding it as a place they can practice in safely. That means 3D modeling, texturing, lighting, and rendering feeding into interactive, user‑centered VR/AR experiences — evaluated the same way any HCI research is evaluated, through usability testing and iteration.

I hold a Master of Science in Modeling, Simulation & Visualization from Purdue University, where my consulting and research work produced four published papers spanning XR design in STEM education, smart‑manufacturing training, biopharma process simulation, and AR accessibility tools for children with autism.
