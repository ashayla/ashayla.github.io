AR Social-Skills Companion for ASD

A mixed-reality application that layers social-skill-building graphics over the real world for children with autism spectrum disorder — applied research published at the 2021 ASEE Virtual Annual Conference.

Overview

This project sits outside the manufacturing-training work: it's an augmented reality graphics application designed to help children with autism spectrum disorder practice social skills, built on ARKit with custom 3D characters and props.

It was developed as part of published research — “Augmented Reality-Based Graphics Application to Assist Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder” (Williams & Chandramouli, ASEE 2021) — grounding the design choices in outcomes for the learners it was built for, not just technical novelty.
