Equipment Operation Trainer — Module 01

A high-fidelity 3D equipment model built for hands-on manufacturing education inside a headset — part of an ongoing research thread on affordable VR/AR training for smart manufacturing.

Overview

This module set the foundation for the equipment-training pipeline: taking a real piece of manufacturing equipment and rebuilding it in full 3D fidelity, then lighting and rendering it so surface detail and operating controls read clearly at headset resolution.

The model was built for Unity and deployed to Oculus, with the training use case in mind from the first block-out — the geometry, texture density, and lighting were all tuned for a learner walking around and interacting with the equipment in VR, not just viewing a static render.
